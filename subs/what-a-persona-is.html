This chapter is not advice for your situation.

In 30 seconds

  • A persona is a role. She isn’t a friend, an employee or a person you can check.
  • Her hard lines belong in writing, and consent comes before everything else.
  • Nobody knows who’s behind her, so every claim about age, gender or place is unverified.
  • Warmth can be honest and can also be what the business runs on.
  • Keep one account fully yours, and watch whether the act is steering your spending.

The hard lines of a persona

Limits belong in the persona file, and not only in someone’s head. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. One example reads: the persona never meets anyone, appears on camera or uses a voice. Nothing in her looks or setting is child-like, whatever the story says. She doesn’t chase, and she never crosses a sub’s real limits. That’s one example, and it isn’t a model for every dom.

What not to share

Red flags

Giving access

  • You hand over a login as proof of trust.
  • You let her read your private conversations with others.
  • A chat stays open on a shared or unlocked computer.

Counter. Keep at least one account fully yours. If she asks for proof, show only a send confirmation with names, balance and numbers hidden. Never show a login or an export of private chats. Change any password you shared. Lock the screen or close the chat.

A sub who gives a persona access to everything has no private space left, and in my experience he acts differently when he knows he’s being watched. A login typed into a chat breaks your security and your anonymity at once, because anyone who can read the chat or the mailbox can use it. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. Keeping your head

Notice the loop early

  1. 1

    After a send, name what you feel: relief, regret, shame.

  2. 2

    Listen for the sentence “better this dom than a stranger”.

  3. 3

    If you want to send less, count the weeks since your last send.

  4. 4

    Tell one person you trust, and do it soon.

  5. 5

    If you can’t stop, go to The spiral and Where to get help.
