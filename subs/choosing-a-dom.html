Step 1 of 6 What to check before you send

This chapter is not advice for your situation.

In 30 seconds

  • Read a persona’s rules and what she says about money before you send anything.
  • A written limit is only a promise. Test it by how she takes a no.
  • Passwords, login codes and remote sessions are never part of the game.
  • Deadlines, guilt and “only me” are pressure, however warm they sound.
  • If you’re already in debt or can’t stop, don’t make this choice alone.

You will learn

  • What a serious persona shows before she asks for money.
  • Which requests and pressures to refuse straight away.
  • How to test a persona without risking money.
  • What to ask before you install a tool made by a dom.
  • Why a dom who earns from you isn’t the person to help you quit.

A picture costs nothing to draw. Rules can be tested, so they cost something. I’d rather you leave early and on good terms than stay because I was nice to you.

Test what she does

A page can be faked, and a dom can say she doesn’t want you to go bankrupt while still pushing you to send. Arousal holds people more than politeness does, so being hooked by a persona is no sign she’s safe. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. Watch what she does after a no.

Red flags

Red flags

Access is never part of the game

  • She asks for a password, a login code or a remote-desktop session.
  • She calls it proof of honesty, trust or forgiveness.
  • She promises to do nothing harmful and reminds you that you can quit.

Counter. Refuse. A real dom needs no proof from you. If she asks for proof anyway, show only a send confirmation with names, balance and numbers hidden. Never show a login or an export of private chats. If you’ve already shared a password, change it now.

A spoken promise doesn’t protect you. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. A US cybersecurity agency advises people not to give control of a computer or phone to a stranger. What you confide, and who you share with

A serious dom keeps what you tell her private. She doesn’t repeat it to anyone else, use it as proof in a fight between doms, or show it to a trainee without your consent. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. Press accounts describe the threat of exposure as a common punishment from financial doms. ReportedStated by a serious secondary source read in full.Diary of a Financial Dominatrix, part one (opens in a new tab) Accessed 3 November 2026. A dom who uses your secrets as leverage has told you what she is.

  • Myth

    If I tell her everything, she’ll keep it to herself.

    Fact

    Only trust a dom with your secrets once she’s shown she keeps them, and a confession is easy to turn into control. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    Two doms don’t mean more sending, because I send the same total anyway.

    Fact

    A second dom adds a second source of pressure on the same wallet. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A dom who says she cares won’t push me.

    Fact

    Care and pressure can sit side by side. Judge what she asks and rewards. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Exclusivity has to be agreed out loud. A dom who wants it has to say so early and get your yes, and two doms can’t share you or hand you over by deciding it between themselves. When you’re in debt

A sub who’s deep in debt, or who can’t stop, can’t choose well, and the right answer is outside help. A responsible dom asks for nothing more while a sub is in debt, stops the money part, and points him to independent debt advice. A dom who offers to help you quit has a conflict of interest, since she earns from your spending. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. Read The spiral and Where to get help.

Check yourself

  1. Question 1 of 3A persona says she needs your login and a code to forgive you. What do you do?

    Answer. Refuse, because a real dom needs no proof from you. Access to your accounts is never part of the game, and asking for it is a red flag.

  2. Question 2 of 3What’s the best test of a persona?

    Answer. What she does after you say no. A page and a manner can both be faked. How she takes a refusal is the evidence.

    Answer. Stop asking for money that evening. Saying money is short pauses the money.
