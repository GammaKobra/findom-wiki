Scams Chapter 2

Stolen art and persona theft

Last checked 3 November 20268 minutes to read

A drawn persona is made of art, words, a look and sometimes a voice, and every part has an owner. This chapter is for the creator who is copied and for the sub who must tell a copy from the real persona.

VerifiedChecked against the original source.ReportedStated by a serious secondary source read in full.Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.UnverifiedPlausible, not proven yet.

This chapter is not advice for your situation.

In 30 seconds

  • Every part of a persona has an owner: art, name, look, words, tools and voice.
  • Borrowing an idea is normal. Reusing a picture needs a yes. Copying is theft.
  • A studio’s character stays the studio’s, and a real person’s face or voice needs consent.
  • Files that a client can open can be copied, so keep dated proof of your own work.
  • A sub should trust the links on the persona’s own pages, never the art alone.

You will learn

  • What belongs to whom when a persona is built.
  • The difference between borrowing, asking and copying.
  • Why studio characters and cloned voices carry real risk.
  • What to do when your work, your name or your picture is copied.
  • How to tell a copy of a persona from the real one.

Everything about me was drawn, written or made by someone, so I treat art as work with an owner. A beautiful character proves nothing about who owns her pictures or who runs her. This page is for the creator whose work gets copied, and for the sub who has to know which account is the one he chose.

Borrow, ask or copy

Borrow an idea. A mood, a pose, a format. Ideas travel across this scene within weeks. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Ask. Reusing a picture needs a yes from its owner, each time. Personas lend each other art, tease each other’s subs with it and post the same trend in both their looks, and permission is what makes it a collab. An artist-persona is often flattered to be asked. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Copy. Taking an image, a post or a tool and presenting it as yours is theft. The fix is cheap: make a similar picture of your own persona. A borrowed picture also tends to miss the proportions your followers know, and they notice. A tool that paints a variant of another artist’s illustration makes a derivative of her work, so such tests stay private and unposted. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    It is only copying if I change a few words or details.

    Fact

    A near copy with small edits is still a copy, and its author will recognise it. Making something of your own is the only safe change. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A creator whose business I dislike has no claim to her work.

    Fact

    Her business does not move the ownership of what she made. The same argument would remove yours. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A “do not steal” label on assets is decoration.

    Fact

    The label is the owner’s request. Each copy that ignores it makes the next one easier to excuse. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

When someone else makes or uses your art

A persona’s art often comes from helpers: an artist, a friend who generates locally, another dom’s pictures. The maker may think of the images as theirs and expect credit, while the persona’s owner shows them as her body. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. Settle it before the images spread.

Before the first image

  1. 1

    Say who owns the finished images and where each of you may use them.

  2. 2

    Say how the maker is credited, or that no credit is wanted.

  3. 3

    Say whether the images may be reused elsewhere, and by whom.

  4. 4

    Write it down where both of you can read it.

Images lent for a persona stay lent: do not share them with anyone else without permission, and ask before reusing one for a new post. Work made for a dom for free needs terms too. The maker chooses the scope, can refuse without losing his place, and is thanked. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

The same care applies to fan-made work. When a sub or fan makes content with a persona, agree first on what may be used and where it may go. The owner of a character may also ask that a product built on her not use her signature default look. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

If you are copied

Whatever a client can open, a determined person can read. Images on a page can be taken with a screenshot. Scrambling files inside an app or extension is weak, because the means to read them travels with the app, and plain files in an archive can be opened by anyone. Light protection still stops casual copies. Do not rely on it. Ship only what you could bear to see copied, and strip metadata from images before posting. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Nobody can prove authorship for you afterwards. Keep dated drafts, layered files, generation settings and any agreement with a maker as you go. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Fakes aimed at you

A drawn persona has no real face to fake, which is a layer of protection. Fakes still reach people: fake explicit pictures made to look like a real person, sent to frighten or to coerce, and edited screenshots sent to a sub to turn him against a dom. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Red flags

If someone threatens you with fake pictures

  • A demand for money, access or silence to keep pictures private.
  • A deadline.
  • A claim that the pictures are real, with nothing you can check.

Counter. Do not pay and do not negotiate. Keep the messages, links and dates. Report the account and the pictures through the platform’s own form, and tell someone you trust. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Check yourself

  1. Question 1 of 2Two accounts show the same art. Which one is the persona you chose?

    Answer. The one that her own link page lists. Art and follower counts are easy to copy. The links she controls are the anchor.

  2. Question 2 of 2A persona says her account was banned and gives a new payment link by message. What do you do?

    Answer. Wait and check through a channel you trusted before the message. A real move can wait. A copy needs you to act before you check.
