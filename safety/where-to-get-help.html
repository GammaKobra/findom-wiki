If someone is in danger right now

A message that says goodbye, or hints at self-harm, is a crisis. That’s no time for a persona, a game or a negotiation. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  1. 1

    Call the emergency number of the country where the person is. The number depends on where they are, not on where you are.

  2. 2

    If that’s another country, or you don’t know where the person is, a call from your phone may not connect. Use the platform’s self-harm report tool, look up that country’s police line on an official site, and send the person a plain message with a local line from the next section.

  3. 3

    Stay on the line or in the room. If you’re far away, ask someone physically near to go.

  4. 4

    Say plainly that you’re worried, and ask whether the person is safe. Don’t argue, bargain or test them.

  5. 5

    Ask for nothing, whether it’s money, a promise or a reply to a game.

  6. 6

    Once help is on its way, tell a person you trust what happened, and use a crisis line from the next section for your own support.

If you’re the one in danger, tell someone physically near you or call the emergency number tonight. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Red flags

When an offer of help isn’t help

  • It asks you to pay first, or to send a tribute to “unlock” help.
  • It asks for a password, a login code, a card number or control of your device.
  • It comes from the dom, the persona or the account you spend on.
  • It promises to get a send back.
  • It wants an answer today.

Counter. Close it. Call a service from the tables above, or a free, non-profit debt adviser.

These signs come from watching how pressure works in the scene. Nobody who helps needs your password, and a real dom never needs it either. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

What to say in one sentence

  1. 1

    Say who you are in one line, or skip it.

  2. 2

    Say the problem in one sentence: I’m sending money online that I can’t stop sending, and I’d like to talk about it.

  3. 3

    Say what you need: to get through tonight, a plan for a debt, or someone to listen.

  4. 4

    You don’t have to say findom, name a persona or describe a kink. You can, if it helps.

  5. 5

    Don’t give your password, a login code or access to your device.
