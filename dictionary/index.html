autodrain

Word class: noun

A tool, usually a browser extension, that sends or buys wishlist items for a sub automatically, on a loop, once he starts it. These tools break every time the platform changes its checkout. A safe one comes switched off, does one named thing on one named site and counts only confirmed payments. It shows a running total, enforces a limit set in advance, has a stop that works in every state and can be removed in one click. Hidden totals, locks that take away the stop and overnight modes are pressure designs to spot and refuse, and a safe design often earns less. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
