Red flags

Money left on a rail you don’t own

  • A dom asks you to keep a balance, credit or prepaid amount with her
  • You’re told a payment route is closing soon and pushed to spend what’s left on it
  • The only way to reach the money is a login on someone else’s service

Counter. Keep your money in your own bank, send only what you planned to give, and count any balance on someone else’s rail as already gone. If you’re a dom selling through one rail, spread your income across routes you can name. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

In a race to release, speed comes before safeguards like a limit, a wait and a stop. Safe design often means earning less, so in a race it’s the first thing cut. If an account or a rail disappears

  1. 1

    Keep one page you own, such as a site, that lists where you can be found.

  2. 2

    Keep copies of your own art and text outside any one platform.

  3. 3

    If you’re a sub, don’t keep a balance or prepaid credit with a dom, and expect that she may vanish.

  4. 4

    Write down the date and what you sent, in case you need it later.
