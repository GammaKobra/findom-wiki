What is 2DFD?

Last checked 3 November 20265 minutes to read

2DFD is financial domination led by a drawn, animated or generated character. The dom is fiction. The money, the time and the consent are not.

Why a drawn dom

For a sub, a drawn dom is a fantasy that no real person’s life gets in the way of. She never has a bad day on camera, never ages and has no private life to wonder about. For a dom, the character is a mask that cannot be stolen, and art can be made in volume where photos cannot. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

The same features make a character easy to copy and to fake. In classic findom, press reports describe scammers who use stolen photos of women. What stays real

A tribute buys nothing. A send is a gift for a moment of play. It does not pay for a service, and nobody can make a dom keep a promise made in a scene. Treat every send as gone the moment you make it. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

The money comes from very few people. In my experience, most doms earn little, and most of the money in the scene comes from a small number of subs, often in their worst moments: late at night, after a relapse, in a burst they regret. A business that lives on those moments has a reason to want them back. Keep that in mind when a dom is very warm. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

The harm is real too. Press reports include people who took on debt and second jobs to keep paying, and others who described the emotional damage of being scammed as worse than the money. ReportedStated by a serious secondary source read in full.[a former participant] has seen men left broke and hungry from ‘paying women to talk to them’ (opens in a new tab) Accessed 3 November 2026.Financial Domination Has a Scamming Problem (opens in a new tab) Accessed 3 November 2026.

Consent is what keeps it a game. Findom is a fantasy two adults choose. When one of them can no longer choose, because of debt, because they cannot stop, or because they are drunk or exhausted, it stops being a game. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

The four rules

  1. 1

    Adults only

    Every character is an adult, with nothing child-like in her looks or setting, whatever the story says, and every player is an adult. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  2. 2

    A cap you set first

    Decide the most you will send while you are calm, before you play, and keep it somewhere the moment cannot reach: your bank, a trusted person, a tool that enforces it. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  3. 3

    A stop that always works

    Any tool or game must stop at once when you ask, with no cost and no delay. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  4. 4

    Nothing that opens your life

    Keep your passwords, login codes, remote access, real name and address to yourself. A real dom never needs them. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    Behind a drawn dom there is a woman who looks like her.

    Fact

    The character is a role. Who runs it is unknown, and her life story is part of the fiction. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A big tribute buys you a place, a promise or a reply.

    Fact

    A tribute buys nothing. A dom may be kind, but nothing in the game can be enforced. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A drawn dom is anonymous, so everything around her is anonymous too.

    Fact

    Payment platforms tie a dom’s payout to a real legal identity. Anonymity ends at the payout. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
