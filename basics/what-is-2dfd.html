Why a drawn dom

For a sub, a drawn dom is a fantasy no real person’s life gets in the way of. She never has a bad day on camera, never ages and has no private life to wonder about. For a dom, the character is a mask nobody can steal, and art can be made in volume in a way photos can’t. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

That also makes a character easy to copy and fake. In classic findom, press reports describe scammers using stolen photos of women. What stays real

A tribute guarantees nothing. A tribute is a gift for a moment of play, and once it’s sent, it’s gone. A dom can promise you anything. Nothing forces her to deliver. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Send because you want to. Never because something was promised.

A few people bring in most of the money. In my experience, most doms earn little, and most of the money in the scene comes from a small number of subs, often at their worst: late at night, after a relapse, in a burst they regret. A business that lives on those moments has a reason to want them back. Keep that in mind when a dom is very warm. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

The harm is real. Press reports include people who took on debt and second jobs to keep paying, and others who said the emotional damage of being scammed was worse than losing the money. Consent keeps it a game. Findom is a fantasy two adults choose. When one of them can’t choose anymore, because of debt, because they can’t stop, or because they’re drunk or exhausted, it’s no longer a game. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

The four rules

  1. 1

    Adults only

    Every character is an adult, with nothing childlike in her looks or setting, whatever the story says. Every player is an adult too. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  2. 2

    Set a limit

    Pick your limit while you’re calm, before you play. Then make it real outside your own head: a spending limit on your card, a friend who knows the number, an app that blocks payments above it. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  3. 3

    A stop that always works

    Any tool or game has to stop the moment you ask, at no cost and without delay. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  4. 4

    Your private details stay private

    Keep your passwords, login codes, remote access, real name and address to yourself. A real dom never needs them. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

    Behind a drawn dom there’s a woman who looks like her.

    Fact

    She’s a role. You don’t know who runs her, and her life story is part of the fiction. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A big tribute buys you a place, a promise or a reply.

    Fact

    A tribute guarantees nothing. A dom may be kind, but nothing in the game can be enforced. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A drawn dom is anonymous, so everything around her is anonymous too.

    Fact

    Payment platforms tie a dom’s payout to a real legal identity. Her anonymity ends there. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
