Step 1 of 6 Same exchange, new mask

In 30 seconds

  • Classic findom has a real person behind it, often with photos, video and voice. 2DFD has a drawn or generated character.
  • The drawing protects the dom’s face and voice. It doesn’t protect her payout, and it doesn’t make her anonymous.
  • For the sub, the risk moves from stolen photos to trust: nobody can check a drawing.
  • Counts of sends, followers and pictures say little about money.
  • The money is real in both. Ask early whether a dominant character expects any.

  • What a drawn dom gains and what she gives up.
  • Why counts of sends, followers and pictures mislead.
  • Why the old advice to check a photo doesn’t work anymore.
  • How to tell a dominant character who wants money from one who doesn’t.

What a drawn dom gains and gives up

What she gains

  • There’s no photo, face or voice left behind to leak, recognise or trace. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
  • Content can be made in volume, and a team can keep the character consistent. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
  • The character can be redrawn, retired or replaced. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

What she gives up

  • Trust. Nobody can check a drawing, so everything she says about her life is part of a role. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
  • Cover when she gets paid. Payment companies tie a payout to a legal identity, with documents, a registered activity and a bank account. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
  • Part of the audience. Some followers dislike AI art, and creators say it hurts their reach. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. There’s no data on how much. UnverifiedPlausible, not proven yet.

Showing less doesn’t make her anonymous. A careless line in a bio, a reused username or a payment record can still lead back to a person. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Myth and fact

  • Myth

    More pictures mean more money.

    Fact

    The amount of content tells you how fast a persona works. A few payers carry most of the money, and more art doesn’t bring more payers. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    A dom without a face can’t be traced.

    Fact

    Showing less doesn’t make her anonymous. Payouts, bios and reused names can still point to a person. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

  • Myth

    Generated art is safe because no real face is shown.

    Fact

    A generated character can copy a real person’s look without consent, and a character taken from an existing game carries rights risks. Every persona has to be an adult in looks and setting, whatever the lore says. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
