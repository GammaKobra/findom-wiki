Step 1 of 6 The usual road

In 30 seconds

  • A persona is spread over several platforms: one for reach, one for gifts, one for chat, sometimes one for subscriptions.
  • Each platform has its own rules on adult content and age. Only one of the pages read for this wiki names findom, so read every rule with care.
  • A wishlist treats every send as a gift. Nobody can make a dom keep a promise made in a scene.
  • Payments are the weak point of the whole scene. Money can be frozen and accounts closed.
  • Picking a platform doesn’t keep you safe. Read the rules yourself and keep your own limit.

Red flags

A page that hides what it is

  • A request to describe your payment as something it isn’t.
  • A page that won’t say what it sells, or what your payment is for.
  • An instruction to keep quiet about what a payment is for if anyone asks.

Counter. Refuse, and only pay through a route that shows what it is. A page built to look like something else is built so a payment company won’t see it. When that fails, it usually ends in a frozen balance or a closed account tied to a real identity, and the money in transit can be yours. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
