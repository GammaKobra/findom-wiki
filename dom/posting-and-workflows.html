Spread the risk across accounts

A persona that lives on one account can lose everything at once. Platforms close, freeze and restrict accounts, and a restricted account can lose most of its reach for weeks. The scene calls a search suggestion ban an SSBSSBShort for search suggestion ban on the main social platform: the account stops showing up in search suggestions, so reach drops and a new persona struggles to grow. A rhythm you can keep

In the scene the usual aim is a post a day. Skipping a day or two is fine as long as the next post comes, and forcing output when you’re low gets you nowhere. After a quiet stretch, come back with something striking instead of more noise. Post at the hour that suits your audience’s evening, whatever your own clock says. Video is an occasional highlight, because a clip costs far more to make than a still. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Replies are part of the rhythm. A slow answer to a private message costs you a session, and the pile of replies after a post is one of the biggest time costs of running a persona. Keep active projects to one or two. Step away from the social accounts for a few days when you’re burnt out. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Edits, captions and launches

Captions. A caption has to say one clear thing. If it contradicts itself, or needs a paragraph to explain its visual metaphor, a fresh reader rejects it, so show it to someone outside before you post. An image alone is weak without words, and a picture the audience has already seen is weaker still. Captions written by a machine tend to feel flat. Write two or three sentences yourself and let a tool polish them. A promo image with too much text overloads the reader. A clean picture with the name and one call to click reads best. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Files. An export can leak more than the picture. GPS data, provenance records and generation prompts can travel inside a file, and a browser encoder may add a colour profile by itself. The 2DFD editor, made by Vivian, is designed to strip this data, read the file again and refuse the export if anything is left. That’s what it’s designed to do. It doesn’t certify that it always does it. A teaser and a full version can be exported as a pair, the teaser censored for the public feed, with the same loop length.
