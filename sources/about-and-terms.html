Not advice

Nothing here is financial, legal or medical advice for anyone’s situation. The chapters give landmarks that apply to many people. Terms of use

The wiki names platforms, services, tools, researchers and publications, and it does so only to explain something or to cite a source. It has no affiliation with any of them, no partnership and no endorsement. Their names belong to their owners. Sources keep their authors: the wiki links to them and quotes at most fifteen words, and it never copies a text, an image or a file.
