Step 1 of 6 A tribute buys nothing

This chapter is not advice for your situation.

In 30 seconds

  • A tribute is a gift. It buys no service and no promise, and nobody can make her keep one.
  • A wishlist is a price menu. The names and prices on it shape what you send.
  • Fees, names and messages follow the platform’s rules, not the dom’s.
  • Pressure during a send evening follows patterns, and each one has an answer.
  • Pick your number before the evening, make it real outside your own head, and keep a way to stop that always works.

You will learn

  • What a tribute, a send, a cash gift and a wishlist are.
  • What the wishlist platform’s own help pages say about gifts, fees and privacy.
  • How a send evening goes, and which nudges to refuse.
  • How to set a limit that holds, and how to raise it safely.
  • Which kinds of tribute aren’t money.

Everything in this chapter comes down to one habit: you pick your number before the evening, never during it.

The wishlist ladder and what it costs

A wishlist is a price menu. Items have themed names and sit on a ladder from a token amount to a very large one. In my experience a ladder runs from about USD 1.00 to about USD 1,000.00, with one gift and one link per rung. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

How a send evening pushes you

A send evening has a set shape, and knowing it protects you more than anything else. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. There’s more in How an evening goes.

What you’ll see from your side

  1. 1

    The dom opens with a teaser or a short command.

  2. 2

    She posts one item link at a time, sometimes with an image.

  3. 3

    You click, pay and confirm.

  4. 4

    She answers with attention: praise, a pet name, an image.

  5. 5

    The next link arrives.

She sets the pace, and the reward often comes late or only partly. That little delay keeps you waiting for the next send. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. Techdom and Games and casino play are built on the same rhythm.

Set a limit and keep it

Your limitlimitA spending limit picked by the person who pays, while calm, before the session. It’s held outside your own head, in a server that enforces it, a bank limit or a trusted person, and it only goes up after a cooldown. Set a limit that holds

  1. 1

    Pick the number while you’re calm, from money you could lose without missing it. Never from borrowed money, rent or savings.

  2. 2

    Add the fees, so the number is the total that leaves your account.

  3. 3

    Make the limit real outside your own head: a bank limit, a separate account, or a person you trust.

  4. 4

    Raise it only after a waitcooldownA fixed wait that has to pass before a decision to spend, or to raise a limit, kicks in. It lets a calm decision overrule an excited one, and a day is a good length for any decision pushed by a deadline. A tool can enforce a cooldown as a minimum gap between payments. of a night or more, never during an evening.

  5. 5

    Know how you’ll stop before you start: close the chat, mute the dom, leave the page. It has to cost nothing and work straight away.

If you run a wishlist, you have your part to do. Name your rungs honestly, don’t keep a public tally of sends, never name a sub’s balance, and end the money part when a sub says money is short.
