Step 1 of 4 Test before you trust

In 30 seconds

  • The big models are close in power. Pick the one whose answers you’d actually post, after testing it.
  • Read a provider’s rules before you use it for adult work. Most of them forbid explicit sexual text.
  • Prices go from a few cents to tens of dollars per million tokens, so the same job can cost very different amounts.
  • Two versions of the same model can behave differently. Test the one you’ll really use.
  • Never paste your legal name, your payment details or a sub’s private messages into a chat.

You will learn

  • Why testing beats any ranking.
  • What each big provider’s rules say about adult text.
  • What the main models cost, by the million tokens and by the month.
  • What to keep out of an AI chat.

Test before you trust

Every big model can write, plan and code well enough for 2DFD work. What changes is how you use it and whether you like its answers. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. A ranking won’t tell you that, so test.

A test you can run in one evening

  1. 1

    Write three requests you really need: a short caption, a calm reply to a sub who asks for more, and a small piece of code.

  2. 2

    Send the same three to two or three models, word for word.

  3. 3

    Read the answers the next day, side by side, without looking at which model wrote which.

  4. 4

    Keep the model whose answers you’d post as they are. Note what it refused, too.

What it costs

What to keep out of the chat

What you paste into a chat leaves your computer. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. Keep these out:

Never paste

  1. 1

    Your legal name, your address, or a document that shows them.

  2. 2

    Payment details, passwords, keys or login codes.

  3. 3

    A sub’s private messages, real name or money details.

  4. 4

    Screenshots that show your other accounts or your desktop.

Use the persona’s name and made-up examples instead. The model doesn’t need the real thing to help. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study. See Stay anonymous.

Your first model

You want an AI to help with captions and a small wishlist tool. A friend says one model is “the best”.

Remember it

  1. 1The big models are close. Test, then pick the answers you like.
  2. 2Read the provider’s rules before adult work.
  3. 3Prices differ a lot: check the API price for anything you build.
  4. 4Never paste real names, payment details or a sub’s private data.
