Step 1 of 5 Plan your month

In 30 seconds

  • A free chat plan is enough to start with captions and advice.
  • A monthly plan suits steady work. Paying per image or per second suits what you make now and then.
  • Video is priced by the second, so the length of your clips makes the bill.
  • A graphics card for local images is a one-time cost, kept apart from the monthly total.
  • Prices change often, and the planner sends nothing anywhere.

You will learn

  • How the planner picks a set of tools for your amount.
  • Which costs are monthly and which depend on how much you make.
  • Which plans stay out of the total, and why.
  • What local images ask of your computer.

How to read it

Each option gets a strength for each need: basic, good or strong. That rating is mine, from using them, not the provider’s. When two sets are as strong as each other, the cheaper one wins. It also names the cheapest set that covers the same needs, because you rarely need the strongest one. When nothing fits, the planner shows the cheapest set and how far over your amount it goes. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.

Images and video can be paid per use. Type how many images or seconds of video you make in a month, and the planner multiplies. One clip of 15 seconds a day is 450 seconds a month, so the per-second price matters more than it looks. Vivian’s field notesFirst-hand experience, not a study.
